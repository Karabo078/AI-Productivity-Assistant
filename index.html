PROMPT ENGINEERING

Better prompts produce better results

The project uses a structured approach: define the role, provide context, state the task clearly, specify constraints, and describe the desired output.

1. RoleTell the AI what role it should perform.
2. ContextGive relevant background information.
3. TaskState exactly what you need.
4. ConstraintsSet tone, length, audience, or format.
5. OutputExplain what the final answer should look like.
RESPONSIBLE AI

AI should assist people, not replace judgment

🔐 Privacy

Avoid entering confidential or sensitive workplace information into public AI tools.

✓ Accuracy

Review AI-generated content before using it, especially for important decisions.

⚖️ Bias

Question outputs that may contain unfair assumptions or incomplete perspectives.

👤 Human oversight

Keep a human responsible for final decisions and published information.
